About our company

Landscaping rooted in trust, craft, and lasting curb appeal.

We help local homeowners and businesses protect their properties, elevate outdoor spaces, and enjoy easier seasonal maintenance.

Our story

From weekend lawn care to a full-service landscaping team.

Green Horizon Landscaping began with a small mower, a few neighborhood clients, and a belief that dependable service should feel simple. Today, our team provides lawn maintenance, planting, seasonal cleanup, and hardscaping for residential and commercial properties.

Our work is guided by careful planning, clean job sites, and honest communication from estimate to final walkthrough.

Mission

To create healthy, functional outdoor spaces that customers are proud to come home to.

We combine practical landscape knowledge with attentive service so every project feels well-managed and built to last.
